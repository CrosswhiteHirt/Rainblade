Interactive Wuxia Mystery

Rainblade:
Three Hidden Truths

One stormy night. One broken blade. Three people hiding the truth.

When a mountain road collapses in a violent storm, you find shelter at Rainfall Inn. At midnight, every lantern goes dark. When the light returns, a broken blade has vanished—and no one can leave.

Investigate the inn, question its three strangers, study the clues they leave behind, and decide who deserves your trust. Every choice brings you closer to the truth of a swordsman who disappeared ten years ago.

StoryFive chapters of rain-soaked mystery.
ChoicesShape relationships and three endings.
PlayExplore clues, dialogue, and simple puzzles.
PlatformLandscape play for iPhone and iPad.

Choose What the Rain Reveals

Will you reveal a secret to the world, put an old sword to rest, or let the past break everything it touches? The final decision is yours.
