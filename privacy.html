Privacy Policy

Effective date: July 17, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how Rainblade: Three Hidden Truths (the “Game”) handles information. The Game is provided by hengbozail (“we”, “us”, or “our”).

Information Stored on Your Device

The Game stores story progress, unlocked chapters and endings, clues, puzzle states, and music, sound, and vibration preferences locally on your device. This information is used to provide saved-game and settings features. You can reset story progress from the Game’s settings. We do not operate an account system or request your name, email address, contacts, photos, or precise location for gameplay.

Advertising and App Tracking Transparency

The Game may show rewarded advertisements. After the Game becomes active, it may request permission through Apple’s App Tracking Transparency framework. If you grant permission, the device advertising identifier may be available to advertising partners for personalized advertising and ad measurement. If you decline or limit permission, the Game continues to work; HSSecurity and advertising setup still run, but an IDFA is not provided by the Game.

Advertising inventory and ad-unit configuration are requested from our configuration service. The Game may initialize supported advertising SDKs only when valid configuration is returned. The integrated advertising SDKs include Chartboost, Yandex Mobile Ads, Vungle, and InMobi. Those providers may collect or process information according to their own privacy policies when advertisements are loaded or displayed.

Security Service

On physical iOS devices, the Game initializes the HSSecurity SDK after the ATT request has completed. This service is used for app security and integrity-related functions. It is not initialized on the iOS Simulator.

Data Sharing and Retention

We do not sell your personal information. Local game data remains on your device unless you reset it or remove the Game. Data handled by advertising or security providers is subject to their respective policies and retention practices.

Children’s Privacy

The Game is not directed to children under the age at which consent is required by applicable law. We do not knowingly collect personal information directly from children.

Changes to This Policy

We may update this policy when the Game or applicable requirements change. The effective date above will be updated when changes are posted.

Contact
